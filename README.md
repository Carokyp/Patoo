## A Wholesome Match-3 Adventure Inspired by My Dog

Patoo is a cosy, colourful match-3 puzzle game inspired by my dog, Poppy (Patoo).
Designed with a bright and playful art style, the game captures the simple happiness of a dog who loves chasing leaves.

Players match different types of leaves to score points, trigger satisfying combos, and progress through gentle, relaxing levels. The game is easy to pick up, calming to play, and perfect for quick breaks or mindful moments.

At its heart, Patoo is a tribute to my mum, who loved video games and introduced me to them.
